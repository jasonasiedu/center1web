ABOUT

CENTER 1 IS AN INDEPENDENT DESIGN PRACTICE BASED IN NEW YORK, WORKING ACROSS FASHION AND OBJECTS. THE PRACTICE IS DEFINED BY AN INTEREST IN MATERIAL, CRAFTSMANSHIP, AND THE DEVELOPMENT OF ALTERNATIVE APPROACHES TO DESIGN.

MATERIALS

MATERIAL RESEARCH IS CENTRAL TO CENTER 1. THE PRACTICE WORKS WITH BIO-BASED LEATHERS AND ALTERNATIVE MATERIALS, EXAMINING THEIR PROPERTIES, LIMITATIONS, AND POTENTIAL THROUGH HANDLING, FABRICATION, AND APPLICATION.

PRINCIPLES

CENTER 1 APPROACHES DESIGN THROUGH EXPERIMENTATION, WITH AN EMPHASIS ON PROCESS, FUNCTION, AND FORM. EACH PROJECT BEGINS WITH AN INVESTIGATION INTO HOW MATERIALS CAN BE USED, ALTERED, AND RECONTEXTUALIZED OUTSIDE CONVENTIONAL METHODS OF MAKING.
